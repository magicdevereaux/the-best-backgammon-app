## 1. What the App is

The App is a backgammon game. It supports:

- **Hotseat play** — two people sharing one device.
- **Online play** — via a shareable game link or code, or by joining a game from
  the open-games list.
- **Accounts** — an optional username and password, which lets your games and
  statistics follow you across devices.
- **Single games and matches** to a target number of points, with gammon and
  backgammon scoring and the doubling cube (including the Crawford rule in
  matches).

The App is free. There are no purchases, subscriptions, advertising, or in-app
currency. There is **no wagering, betting, or gambling of any kind**, and no
prizes of monetary value. You must not use the App to conduct gambling.

There is no chat or messaging feature, and no automated matchmaking — you play
people you deliberately invite or whose open game you join.
